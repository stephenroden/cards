Hearts Rules

  1. Follow suit if you can.
  2. Hearts cannot be led until broken.
  3. Hearts are worth 1 point; queen of spades is 13.
  4. Optional variant: jack of diamonds is worth -10 points.
  5. Shooting the moon scores 26 points to others.
} @else if (gameId() === 'blackjack') {

Blackjack Rules

  1. 4 seats: you and 3 CPUs, each betting its own chips against one dealer from a six-deck shoe.
  2. Cards count face value, faces are 10, and an ace is 11 until that would bust.
  3. A natural 21 on the first two cards pays 3:2; any other win pays even money.
  4. Double takes exactly one more card for a matching bet; split pairs into two hands.
  5. Split aces get one card each, and 21 after a split is not a natural.
  6. Insurance is offered on a dealer ace, costs half your bet, and pays 2:1.
  7. Surrender on your first decision returns half the bet.
  8. The dealer draws to 17 and stands on soft 17 unless the variant is enabled.
  9. You act first, then the CPU seats play basic strategy, then the dealer draws.
} @else {

Poker Rules (Texas Hold'em)

  1. 4 players: you and 3 CPUs. Each hand deals 2 hole cards.
  2. Fixed-limit betting: 2-chip bets preflop/flop, 4-chip bets turn/river.
  3. Small blind is 1, big blind is 2, and the button rotates each hand.
  4. Best five-card hand from seven cards wins at showdown.
  5. Session continues until one player has all chips.
}
